## 5. Design direction

The concept is "The Inflection": ivory paper, ink type, deep green for authority, and one signature motif — a line that bends upward and a prism that turns white light into a spectrum. It says "ideas become impact" without a word, and it grows straight out of his Beyond Ideas cover.

### Colour

Contrast ratios were checked against the ivory background (WCAG AA needs 4.5:1 for body text).

| Token | Hex | Use | Contrast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ivory paper | #F6F1E7 | Page background, echoes the book banner | — |
| Ink | #1C1F1E | Headings and body text | 14.8:1 on ivory |
| Institution green | #1F3D33 | Nav, footer, buttons, section bands | 10.5:1 on ivory; ivory text on green 10.5:1 |
| Stone | #6B665C | Captions, dates, secondary text | 5.1:1 on ivory |
| Brass | #B08D57 | Thin rules, icons, large decorative numerals only — never text | 2.75:1, decorative only |
| Light brass | #D4B483 | Small accent text on green bands | 6.0:1 on green |

The spectrum (red → violet) appears only inside the prism motif and on the Beyond Ideas book section. Everywhere else stays ivory, ink and green.

### Type

- **Headings:** Cormorant Garamond, 600–700. Close to the classic serif on his book cover. Use it only at 28px and above; it gets thin when small.
- **Body and UI:** Source Sans 3, 18px minimum, line height 1.6, 60–72 characters per line. Comfortable for a senior audience.
- **Numerals:** Cormorant Garamond in brass for the numbers strip, large.
- Both are free Google Fonts; self-host them through Fontsource so the site doesn't wait on Google.

### Imagery

- A new professional portrait is the single biggest upgrade: 2–3 shots, warm light, one in a library or classroom, one holding both books. The current LinkedIn photo is too small for a hero.
- Real event photos (Navy sessions, Kampala, classrooms), colour-graded warm and slightly desaturated so they sit together.
- No stock photos. Book covers as 3D mock-ups with a soft shadow.

### Signature motif

- **The inflection line:** a thin brass line that runs flat, then curves upward. It draws itself once in the hero, then reappears as section dividers.
- **The prism:** a small line-art prism; on the Books and Frameworks sections the light passing through it splits into the spectrum.

### Motion

Cinematic and scroll-driven, on institution green and ivory — never black, neon or glitch. The full system is "Motion v2" in section 5b; the motion notes in the 5a table below describe the earlier, subtler version.

## 5b. Motion v2: cinematic, scroll-driven

This section replaces the earlier "subtle by design" motion system. The site keeps its brand — ivory, ink, institution green, brass, Cormorant Garamond and Source Sans 3 — but its motion now has the feel of two references: Muhsin Gigani's portfolio (a signature object that travels through the page, a pinned journey, a numbered work slider) and Sanika Joshi's (a percentage loader, a hero that a paper-like sheet slides over, colour-blocked bands, a scattered collage). The big moments happen on institution green and ivory. Never black backgrounds, neon or glitch effects.

### Engine

- **GSAP** (free npm package) with ScrollTrigger, SplitText, DrawSVGPlugin, Flip (loaded on demand) and CustomEase. No Three.js.
- One engine file, `src/lib/scroll.ts`: registers plugins once; runs **Lenis** on desktop fine pointers only, driven by `gsap.ticker` (`lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update)`, autoRaf off, `lagSmoothing(0)`); wraps every animation in `gsap.matchMedia()` with three contexts — **desktop** `(min-width:1024px) and (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`, **touch/small**, and **reduced motion**.
- Astro ClientRouter: on `astro:before-swap` every context is reverted and every ScrollTrigger killed; on `astro:page-load` they are created again. The engine downloads only after the hero portrait (the LCP image) has painted; component scripts register their scenes through a tiny GSAP-free registry (`src/lib/scenes.ts`, `src/lib/tools.ts`) meanwhile. Start-up runs the on-screen scenes first, then the rest one per task (no long task), then one `ScrollTrigger.refresh()`; it refreshes again after `document.fonts.ready` and after any size-less lazy image loads. Keyboard focus survives a refresh. Back/forward never leaves a stale pin.
- **GSAP owns all motion.** The Motion library is no longer used: the Book3D island became `BookCover.astro` (scroll rise + pointer tilt), and the Testimonials and AchievementLightbox islands animate with GSAP (SplitText lines, a drawn quote mark, Flip). React remains only for those two interactive islands.
- `ScrollTrigger.config({ ignoreMobileResize: true })`; no normalizeScroll.

### Added after the first review

- **Brass thread** (home, desktop): one brass line runs down the left gutter from the manifesto to the footer, bending like the inflection curve at each section; its glowing tip stays ~60% down the screen and each section's numbered node lights as it passes. Drawn once and revealed with a clip, so it costs almost nothing per frame.
- **Opening book**: the Beyond Ideas cover swings open on its spine (scrubbed, ≤100° so it never covers the text) to an inside page carrying the excerpt.
- **Curve-shaped transitions both ways**: green bands begin and end on the inflection curve (manifesto, Books, footer), each bending into shape as it scrolls in.

### Motion v3: three tiers (calmer, faster to read)

Motion v3 (MOTION_V3_PROMPT.md) edits v2 rather than adding to it. One signature motion — the travelling prism — and every other effect sits in one of three tiers:

- **Tier 1 · cinematic, scrubbed (scrub 0.8):** only the prism, the Manifesto (pinned ~100vh), the Arc (year snaps to each entry), the book covers (rise; Beyond Ideas opens) and the Speaking track (pinned horizontal).
- **Tier 2 · section reveals, one-shot as the top crosses 80% of the screen:** SplitText line reveals only on h1 and section h2s (0.9s). Everything else — body copy, cards, small images, h3s, display lines — fades up 16px → 0 over 0.6s (expo-out). Clip-path wipes only on large photos (hero portrait under the first-visit loader, gallery photos, About portrait). Any staggered group finishes its stagger within 0.4s. `fastScrollEnd` and `preventOverlaps`, so a fast scroll completes reveals instantly. Characters are split only on the hero name. On-load reveals wait for the page-transition curtain to lift.
- **Tier 3 · UI feedback:** 0.15–0.2s with the "inflect" ease (hovers, taps, menus, cursor, magnetic buttons).
- **Budgets:** at most 3 long pins on the home page (hero sheet, Manifesto, Speaking), each with `anticipatePin`; no pins on phones; the nav bar always stays in view; loader ≤1.2s and only on a first visit per device; idle loops pause off-screen or when the tab is hidden; Lenis lerp 0.12.

### Motion v4: Onda's motion in our brand

Motion v4 (MOTION_V4_PROMPT.md) takes the scroll effects of the Onda studio template — not its look — and rebuilds them with GSAP, ScrollTrigger and Lenis (no CSS scroll timelines), inside the v3 tiers and budgets.

- **Hero (Onda's aperture):** intro copy top-left, the giant two-line name bottom-left ending in a brass triangle full stop, and on the right an aperture: hairline orbit ring with a brass moon, a brass glow and the line-art prism. Intro: the aperture swells in, the beam draws and the spectrum fans out. On desktop, scrolling shrinks the prism onto the full stop (ring and glow fade, beam and spectrum go out, the glass fills with brass, the intro lifts 70px); the travelling prism then lifts off from that full stop into the Manifesto. Phones, tablets, reduced motion and JS off: the prism stays put and the full stop is static. The name is never hidden before scripts run (it is the LCP element).
- **Pillars (Onda's stacked cards):** four green cards, sticky from 768px, each 14px lower than the last; as a card slides over, the one below shrinks to 0.9 and darkens. Phones and reduced motion: a plain list.
- **Speaking (Onda's selected work):** the pinned horizontal track now sits on a green plate that opens from a rounded inset to full width; after the track has run, the cards tilt and fall out of the plate as it scrolls away.
- **Manifesto (Onda's statement):** the quote's two clauses slide in from opposite sides as the green band arrives, then the words light up across the pin; the brass triangle full stop lands last.
- **Arc (Onda's method):** beside the timeline, under the chapter, a ring draws itself as you read; a runner goes round it and meets each chapter's station as that chapter begins, and the station swells and fills brass. Desktop only.
- **Testimonials (Onda's "again" wheel):** on desktop every quote is a green card on a large wheel; the heading rises and the wheel swings up into place, then scrolling turns it so the cards pass right to left. All quotes are plain text in reading order. Phones, tablets and reduced motion keep the one-quote-at-a-time carousel.
- **Budgets:** at most 3 GSAP pins on home (currently Manifesto and Speaking); sticky stacks are checked like pins for "feels stuck".

### Tokens (CSS in `global.css`, GSAP in `src/lib/motion.ts` → `GS`)

| Token | Value |
| --- | --- |
| UI ease "inflect" | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (registered with CustomEase) |
| Reveal eases | `expo.out` (default), `power4.out` (clip wipes, odometers) |
| Reveal duration | 0.9–1.2s |
| Stagger | characters 0.025s · words 0.04s · lines 0.08s · frames 0.08s |
| Scrub | 0.6–1 (default 0.8) |

| Page transition | ≤ 800ms · loader ≤ 1.8s |
| Limits | magnetic pull ≤ 8px · velocity skew ≤ 3° · tilt ≤ 6° |

### Primitives

- **Split reveal** `[data-split="lines|words|chars"]` — SplitText lines in masks, yPercent 110 → 0. `aria: 'auto'` keeps whole words for screen readers; `autoSplit` + `onSplit` re-split cleanly on resize.
- **Clip reveal** `[data-clip="up|down|left|right"]` — clip-path inset wipe; the inner image scales 1.15 → 1. **Parallax** `[data-parallax]` yPercent ±8, desktop only.
- **Odometer** `[data-odometer]` — rolling digit columns; the real value stays in sr-only text.
- **Magnetic** `[data-magnetic]` — follows the pointer, at most 8px, desktop only.
- **Inflection edge** (`InflectionEdge.astro`) — the top edge of a green band starts flat and bends upward as it scrolls in (scrubbed path).
- **Travelling prism** (`PrismTraveller.astro` + `src/lib/prism.ts`) — the signature graphic on a fixed, aria-hidden layer; scrubbed between `[data-prism-stop]` waypoints (scale, beam, spectrum split, rotation, opacity). Its 6s breathing glow is its only idle loop. Desktop only; phones and tablets get static prisms in the hero and Books.
- **Custom cursor** — an ink dot and a brass ring that follows with lerp, growing with a label ("Read", "View", "Open", "Drag") over cards and photos. Native cursor on inputs. Desktop fine pointers only.

7. **Cursor** and **magnetic primary buttons** (desktop).
8. **Page transitions** — a green curtain wipe carrying the brass inflection line, ≤ 800ms; instant under reduced motion.

### Home page

Phones never pin, except where noted; they get the same intros, swipe rows with counters, and reveals as things enter.

### Guardrails (non-negotiable)

- **JS off:** every word and image is visible. Hidden start states are set only at runtime by GSAP; the `<noscript>` overrides stay.
- **Reduced motion:** no loader, pins, scrub, cursor, marquee or prism travel; Lenis off; content simply appears.
- **Pinned and horizontal sections** stay reachable by keyboard and screen reader in reading order, with no focus traps; tab order matches what is visible.
- Animate only **transform, opacity, clip-path and filter** (plus the scrubbed SVG path of the inflection edge and stroke drawing); `will-change` only while animating; 60fps on a mid-range phone under 4× CPU throttle.
- **Budgets:** about 180KB of JS (gzipped) on the home page, heavy parts starting only near the viewport. Lighthouse mobile: Performance 85+, Accessibility 95+, CLS < 0.1, LCP < 2.5s.

### Rules that make it work everywhere

- Fluid type with `clamp()`, e.g. the hero name runs from about 40px on phones to 88px on desktops without jumps.
- Container queries on cards so a card looks right wherever it is placed.
- Use `100dvh`, not `100vh`, for the hero so mobile browser bars don't cut it off; respect notch safe areas.
- Responsive images with `srcset` and `sizes`; AVIF/WebP; lazy-load everything below the hero; preload the hero image and heading font.
- Touch targets at least 44 × 44px; anything shown on hover also works on tap.
- Turn off tilt, parallax and draw-in effects under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Check phones in landscape too; the hero must not overflow at 667 × 375.

### Quality targets

- WCAG AA: contrast as in section 5, real headings in order, alt text on every photo, visible focus outlines, full keyboard navigation.
- Performance on a mid-range phone over 4G: Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5s, layout shift under 0.1, at most about 120KB of JavaScript on the home page, with the animated parts loading as they scroll into view, Lighthouse 90+.

### Test on

iPhone SE (375px) · iPhone 15 (393px) · a common Android (412px) · iPad portrait (768–820px) · iPad landscape (1024–1180px) · laptop (1366–1440px) · desktop (1920px). Use Chrome DevTools device mode while building, then check on at least one real phone and one real laptop before launch.
